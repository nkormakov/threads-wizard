Outer Ø is the nominal (major) diameter. Inner Ø, bolt is the external thread minor diameter d3 = d − 1.22687·P. Inner Ø, nut is the internal thread minor diameter D1 = d − 1.08253·P. Metric and Unified threads share the same 60° basic profile, so both use these formulas (P = 1/TPI for inch threads). Values are rounded to 3 decimals in mm and 4 decimals in inches. Coarse = UNC and fine = UNF for inch threads. Native units show metric threads in mm and inch threads in inches/TPI.
